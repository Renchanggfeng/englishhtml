高频万能句型总结

全书 120 句其实只反复用了下面 10 个句型，掌握句型再往里填单词即可举一反三。

I'd like (to) …　我想要……全书最高频，点单 / 订房 / 租车 / 看病全靠它
  • I'd like a window seat. / I'd like the beef.　靠窗座位 / 我要牛肉（机上）
  • I'd like a room for two nights. / I'd like to exchange some money.　住两晚的房间 / 兑换些钱
  • I'd like to rent a car. / I'd like to see a doctor.　租车 / 看医生
Can I have / get / see …?　能给我/看看……吗向对方索要东西或服务
  • Can I have a large pizza? / Can I have a round-trip ticket, please?
  • Can I get a city map? / Can I see the menu?
Could you … ?　您能……吗比 Can you 更客气的请求
  • Could you recommend a nice wine?　推荐款好酒
  • Could you give me a discount? / Would you take a picture of me?　打折 / 帮拍张照
How much is / are …?　……多少钱问价格，is 接单数、are 接复数
  • How much is a room for 2 nights? / How much are those?
  • How much are the tickets for two adults?
Where's …? / Which … goes to …?　问路与问线路
  • Where's the outlet mall? / Where's the lost and found?
  • Which train goes to the Central Park? / Which bus goes to the terminal?
I'm here for … / I'm headed to …　表目的与去向
  • I'm here for sightseeing. / I'm here for 5 days.　入境必答
  • I'm headed to Milan.　我去米兰（自驾/问路）
Do you have …?　你们有……吗
  • Do you have a morning tour? / Do you have anything on sale?
Please + 动词　请……直接提出要求
  • Please, keep the change. / Please, use the meter.　不用找零 / 请打表
  • Please speak slowly. / Please speak Chinese.　救命必背
症状三件套：I feel … / I have … / It hurts …就医描述身体状况
  • I feel nauseous. / I feel like fainting.　恶心 / 要晕倒
  • I have a stomachache. / I have a bad headache.
  • It hurts here. / It hurts every 5 minutes. / It hurts when I touch it.
保险专用：Will my insurance …? / How can I get …?
  • Will my insurance cover it?　保险涵盖这项吗
  • How can I get an insurance certificate?　怎么开保险证明
