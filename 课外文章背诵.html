UiPath Agentic Automation Builder Professional CertificationUiPath 智能体自动化构建者专业认证 · 发布公告

来源：自选课外文章/01_UiPath Agentic智能体自动化专业认证发布公告.txt · 收录于 2026/09/26 · 约 300 词
科技产品公告AI 自动化

We're excited to introduce the UiPath Agentic Automation Builder Professional Certification, our newest UiPath Certified Professional credential, launching on September 22 at UiPath Fusion. 我们很高兴地向大家介绍 UiPath 智能体自动化构建者专业认证——UiPath 最新的一项专业认证资质，将于9月22日在 UiPath Fusion 大会上正式发布。

Created for professionals ready to turn the potential of agentic automation into real-world, enterprise-ready solutions, this certification validates the skills needed to build, orchestrate, evaluate, and govern the next generation of automation. 这项认证专为那些准备将智能体自动化的潜力转化为真实的、企业级解决方案的专业人士打造，它验证的是构建、编排、评估和治理下一代自动化所需的技能。

As organizations embrace Agents & Operational AI, building successful solutions requires more than creating individual agents. It means understanding how intelligent, adaptive agents can work alongside Deterministic Automation, combining flexibility and reasoning with the consistency and precision businesses rely on. 随着各组织拥抱智能体与运营式 AI，构建成功的解决方案所需要的不仅仅是创建单个智能体。它意味着要理解：智能的、自适应的智能体如何与确定性自动化协同工作，把灵活性和推理能力与企业所依赖的一致性和精确性结合起来。

The certification validates the ability to use Agentic Business Orchestration to coordinate work across agents, robots, people, and enterprise systems, while leveraging UiPath for Coding Agents as part of a modern automation development experience. 该认证验证这样的能力：使用"智能体业务编排"来协调智能体、机器人、人和企业系统之间的工作，同时将 UiPath 编码智能体作为现代自动化开发体验的一部分加以利用。

And building is only part of the equation. As agentic automation scales across the enterprise, Evaluation, Governance and Reliability become essential to ensuring solutions perform effectively, responsibly, and consistently. 而"构建"只是等式的一部分。随着智能体自动化在企业范围内规模化扩展，评估、治理与可靠性对于确保解决方案有效、负责、一致地运行，变得至关重要。

The certification gives professionals the opportunity to demonstrate that they can go beyond understanding agentic automation and build solutions designed for real-world enterprise impact. 这项认证让专业人士有机会证明：他们能够超越对智能体自动化的"理解"层面，去构建专为真实企业影响力设计的解决方案。

To celebrate the launch, candidates can receive 50% off the certification exam from September 22 through November 22, 2026. 为庆祝发布，考生可在2026年9月22日至11月22日期间享受认证考试五折优惠。
