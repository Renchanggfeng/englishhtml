语法与句型点总结

15 个 Unit 涉及的语法/句型汇总。教材语法体系循序渐进：情态动词 → 疑问句三步 → 进行时与将来时 → 现在完成时 → 比较级/使役。

语法 / 句型要点教材例句出处
would like（'d like）礼貌表达“想要”。would 是情态动词，各人称同形、不加 -sI'd like a return air ticket from Narita to London, please.U1
be 动词I am / You·We·They are / He·She·It is；两大功能：存在（There is…）与系词（A = B）There's one right in front here. / My name is Naomi Abe.U1·U2
疑问句 Part 1（情态动词）陈述句直接把情态动词提前 = Yes/No 问句；前面再加疑问词 = WH 问句Would you like a first class ticket? / When would you like to go?U1
疑问句 Part 2（be 动词）把 be 动词提前构成疑问；否定 not 紧跟 be/助动词之后Is this the check-in for the flight to London? / Where is the check-in desk?U2
疑问句 Part 3（实义动词）一般现在时借助 do/does 构成疑问与否定（does 用于第三人称单数）Do you have a return ticket? / Does he want a room with seaview?U4·U5
疑问词八件套what/who/where/when/which/whose/why/how 分别问信息、身份、地点、时间、选择、所属、原因、方式What time do you start work? / Which one do you want?U1
May I…? / Could you…?May I 请求许可（主语是 I）；Could you 请求对方做事，比 can you 客气May I have your ticket and passport? / Could you put your baggage on this scale?U2
Can I…?询问“我可不可以做某事”Can I take this briefcase as hand baggage?U2·U13
will（将来）谈未来的情态动词；will + be + …ing 表示不久将来的动作We will be landing in about 2 hours. / I'll get one for you.U3·U4
现在进行时be + 现在分词，表示说话时正在进行的临时动作We are waiting for take-off clearance.U3
祈使句 + please祈使句隐含主语 you，动词原形；加 please 变礼貌请求，否定用 Don't；也用于指路Please fasten your seat belts. / Don't inflate it inside the aircraft. / Turn left and keep on the main road.U3·U9·U10
have 作实义动词疑问/否定要借 do/does（英国英语 have got 除外）Do you have a return ticket? / He doesn't have a ticket.U5
be going to表示已计划好的将来How long are you going to stay in the UK?U5
现在完成时have/has + 过去分词：过去发生的动作对现在有影响，或过去的经历；不与过去时间状语连用You've come into the Green Channel. / I've been to Italy.U5
可数 / 不可数名词luggage、baggage、furniture 不可数，无复数，量用 piece 表示Some new pieces of furniture have arrived.U2
比较级与最高级fast–faster–fastest / good–better–best / cheap–cheaper–cheapestWhat's the fastest way to get to central London?U7
强调式 do助动词 do/does 强调谓语动词，表示“确实、真的”We do serve dinner in the Roof Garden.U7
使役动词get/ask/tell/want + sb + to do；have + sb + do（美式更常用）I'll get a porter to take your luggage up. / I'll have a taxi waiting for you.U7·U8
let / help + sb + (to) dolet 后接不带 to 的动词原形；help 的 to 可省Let me help you. / Could you help me put this bag in the overhead compartment?U3·U4
缩写形式'd like = would like；Where's/That's/There's；I'll = I will；you've = you have；isn't = is notI'll have the chicken, please.各单元
