课程速览

原教材：New Daily English（新日常会话），纯英文会话教材，共 3 个级别 × 5 册（A–E）× 10 课 = 150 课。 每课结构固定：Example（3–4 个例句，围绕一个句型）→ Conversation（6–8 句日常对话）→ Grammar Focus（句型公式表）。 3 级以 Serena 一家的起居生活为主线，4 级围绕 Naoto 在加拿大寄宿家庭的生活展开，5 级进入抽象话题与复杂句法。

3 级 · 基础起居起床做家务、乘公交、购物问价、情绪身体、道歉致谢。句型：make + 名词、Did you…、祈使句、like + 动名词、-er than、used to、Have you ever been to…
4 级 · 海外生活寄宿家庭、购物就餐、打国际电话、节日、求职入学。句型：情态动词 should/must/have to、频率副词、现在完成进行、被动语态、不定式、附加疑问、时间定语从句
5 级 · 高阶句法抽象话题讨论（爱好恐惧、职业评价、工作抱怨）。句型：名词+介词+动名词、-ed/-ing 形容词、动名词与不定式句法功能、全套条件句与 wish、定语从句、名词性从句
3个级别15册150课150个核心句型300+例句

语法体系总结

150 课的 Grammar Focus 按语法主题聚类如下，每条给出课号索引与代表句，便于按语法点横向复习。

一般现在时 · 第三人称单数3A1 3A7 3B8 3C5 3C6 3C9He/She/It + 动词-s
  • She makes breakfast. / I make my bed.　她做早餐。/ 我整理床铺。
  • He likes painting. / She plays the piano. / He sweats when he's hot.
一般过去时 · 否定与疑问3A2 3C2 3D10 4E1Did you + 动词原形 / didn't + 动词原形 / 名词 + didn't work
  • Did you take a shower? / Did she clean her room?　你洗澡了吗？/ 她打扫房间了吗？
  • I didn't do my homework. / The water faucet didn't work. / I almost forgot her birthday.
祈使句 · 叮嘱与指示3A4 3A9 3E3 4A7 4A8动词原形开头 / Don't forget to… / Don't be…
  • Don't forget to clear the table. / Go to the supermarket.　别忘了收拾桌子。/ 去超市。
  • Turn down the TV. / Take off your shoes. / Don't be sad.
be / feel / get / look + 形容词 · 表状态3A10 3B3 3B6 3C1 3E6 3E7 4A2 4A4 4C2情绪、身体、天气、外观的最核心表达
  • It's windy today. / I feel so unhappy. / I am hungry.　今天有风。/ 我很难过。/ 我饿了。
  • I got lost. / Looks delicious. / He looks like his father. / You must be busy.
动名词与不定式 · 入门到精通3A7 3B7 3C4 3C10 4A5 4C8 5C1-5C10like + doing → want to do → 动名词/不定式短语作主语、宾语、介词宾语
  • I like swimming. / Let's go shopping. / Swimming is good exercise. / I want to buy a car.
  • Finding a parking space is quite difficult. / To help other people feels good. / Will you allow me to use your telephone?
情态动词 can / should / must / have to / had better3E1 3E2 3E9 4B3 5C10请求、建议、必要性与推测（must be = 想必）
  • Can you recommend something for my headache? / You should take medicine.　能推荐点治头痛的药吗？/ 你该吃药。
  • You must wear an ID. / You had better stop working. / We must keep quiet.
比较级 · 最高级 · the best … ever3C3 4E7-er than / the best + 名词 + I've ever + 过去分词
  • I'm smarter than you. / It's the best concert I've ever been to.　我比你聪明。/ 这是我看过的最棒的演唱会。
经历与完成 · used to / ever been to / for·since / 完成进行3C8 3D2 4B9 4C1过去习惯、人生经历、持续时间、持续至今的动作
  • I used to work in a fast-food restaurant. / Have you ever been to Mt. Fuji?　我过去在快餐店打工。/ 你去过富士山吗？
  • I've lived there since 2008. / He has been working two jobs.
副词 · 频率与程度4B2 4B5 4E3always/often/sometimes/never；very/fairly/really/extremely；First/Then/Next/Finally
  • I always meet my friends here. / I never smoke. / Canada is fairly big.　我总在这见朋友。/ 我从不抽烟。/ 加拿大相当大。
被动语态与分词作形容词4C5 4D2 4D8 5B1-5B10was written by / need to be done / -ing 与 -ed 形容词之辨
  • Mona Lisa was painted by Leonardo da Vinci. / The windows need to be cleaned. / The glass is cracked.
  • The new manager is frightening. / I'm frightened about meeting her.　新经理让人害怕。/ 要见她我很害怕。
名词 + 介词 + 动名词5A1-5A5love of / talent for / fear of / addiction to / reason for + doing
  • My fear of flying made traveling difficult. / His addiction to smoking has become serious.　对飞行的恐惧让旅行变得困难。/ 他的烟瘾已经很严重了。
形容词 + 介词搭配5A6-5A10kind to / tired of / disappointed with / famous for / good at
  • She's friendly to everyone. / I'm disappointed with myself. / I'm good at English.　她对谁都友好。/ 我对自己很失望。/ 我英语很好。
条件句全套与 wish4D9 5D1-5D10零条件→一条件→二条件→三条件→混合条件→虚拟愿望
  • Plants die if they don't get enough water.（零）/ If it rains, I will stay at home.（一）
  • If I were not busy, I would come to a party.（二）/ If I'd known you were coming, I would have baked a cake.（三）
  • I wish she would stop complaining. / I wish I were a doctor.　我希望她别再抱怨了。/ 我真希望自己是医生。
从句体系 · 定语从句 / 名词性从句 / 附加疑问4D4 4E8 4E9 4E10 5E1-5E10who/that/where/when 定语从句；what/that 名词性从句五功能；反义疑问与附和
  • The woman who lives next door is my English teacher. / That restaurant is an expensive place where rich people eat.
  • What he said was a lie. / She said that she was feeling sick. / The fact that the earthquake destroyed a lot of buildings is affecting the town.
  • It is an interesting movie, isn't it? / I like traveling. — So do I.
疑问句全家桶 · 特殊问句与双宾语3A5 3B1 3B2 3B9 3B10 3D3 4B1 4B8 4D5 4D7 4E4Who/What/Where/When/How much/How long/How + adj；主语+动词+间宾+直宾
  • What does he do? / How much is this? / How do I get to the train station? / How high is Mt. Fuji?
  • I sent you a birthday card. / She told him the news.　我寄了张生日卡给你。/ 她把消息告诉了他。
