Jobs and Occupations 工作与职业

Intermediates D1 · 俚语课Beginners D1▶

◆ 核心词汇

big bucks/bɪɡ bʌks/一大笔钱
daily grind/ˈdeɪli ɡraɪnd/日常苦差、单调工作
top dog/tɑːp dɔːɡ/大人物、一把手
happy hour/ˈhæpi ˌaʊər/(酒吧)饮品打折时段
chicken feed/ˈtʃɪkɪn fiːd/一小笔钱(与 big bucks 反义!)

◆ 教材关键句

  1. I'm earning big bucks at my new job. I'll finally be able to afford that new car! (afford sth 买得起)我在新工作赚大钱，终于买得起那辆新车了！
  2. Brad has been doing the same job for thirty years. He's tired of the daily grind! (be tired of 厌倦)Brad 做同一份工作三十年了，他厌倦了这种日复一日的苦差！
  3. Carlos got promoted. Now he's a top dog! (get promoted 升职)Carlos 升职了，现在他是一把手了！
  4. After weeks of finishing the project, we can go to happy hour and get a few drinks!项目完结后，我们可以去酒吧特价时段喝几杯！
  5. Before I got promoted, I was working for chicken feed!升职之前，我挣的钱少得可怜！
⚠️ 原PDF印成 "he'sa top dog" 是印刷错误，应为 he's a top dog。big bucks ↔ chicken feed 反义组，一对记忆！

◆ Beginners 基础补充 (D1)

  • nurse 护士 · firefighter 消防员 · policeman 警察 · teacher 教师
  • Firefighters put out fires and rescue people.消防员灭火并救人。
  • I have thought of becoming a policeman. (现在完成时)我曾想过当警察。
  • I think teachers do a tough job.我觉得教师的工作很辛苦。

◆ 扩展词汇

  • 钱相关: make a living 谋生 · breadwinner 养家人 · loaded 富得流油 · strapped for cash 手头紧 · get a raise 涨薪
  • 职场: climb the corporate ladder · nine-to-five 朝九晚五 · work overtime 加班 · dead-end job 没前途的工作 · job-hopping 跳槽 · the rat race 都市竞争 · lay off 裁员
  • 人物: colleague 同事 · supervisor 上司 · employee↔employer 员工↔雇主 · freelancer 自由职业 · workaholic 工作狂

◆ 扩展语句

  1. I'm tired of the daily grind — I really need a vacation.我厌倦了每天的苦差事——我真需要休假。
  2. He earns big bucks working as a software engineer.他当软件工程师赚大钱。
  3. She worked for chicken feed when she first started her career.她刚入行时挣的钱少得可怜。
  4. After ten years of hard work, he finally became the top dog in the company.经过十年的努力，他终于成了公司的一把手。
  5. My coworkers and I often grab drinks during happy hour on Fridays.周五下班后，我和同事们常在酒吧特价时段喝一杯。
  6. I'd rather have a job I love than earn big bucks doing something boring.我宁愿做自己热爱的工作，也不愿赚大钱做无聊的事。
  7. Working overtime every day is bad for your health.每天加班对你的健康有害。
  8. Many young people are tired of the rat race and want a slower life.很多年轻人厌倦了激烈竞争，想要慢节奏的生活。
  9. She quit her nine-to-five job and became a freelancer.她辞掉了朝九晚五的工作，成了自由职业者。
  10. My father was the breadwinner of the family, so he never stopped working.我父亲是家里的顶梁柱，所以他从不停止工作。
  • 💬 What matters more to you: a high salary or job satisfaction?对你来说，高薪和工作满意度哪个更重要？
  • 💬 Would you take a promotion if it meant much more stress?如果升职意味着压力更大，你还会接受吗？

◆ 口语练习

  • What do you do for a living?
  • Would you rather earn big bucks with a daily grind, or earn chicken feed doing what you love?
02

Stress 压力

Intermediates D2 · 仅4词▶

◆ 核心词汇

stressed out/strest aʊt/焦虑疲惫、绷不住了
retire/rɪˈtaɪər/退休
stress/stres/精神压力
nap/næp/小睡(尤指白天)

◆ 教材关键句

  1. You know you are stressed out. Give yourself some time to relax.你知道你已经压力过大了，给自己一点放松的时间。
  2. I get a lot of stress from my coworkers. They are very competitive. (competitive 竞争激烈)同事给我很大压力，他们竞争心很强。
  3. I feel tired during the day. I take a nap.我白天犯困，就小睡一会儿。
  4. Mark stopped working last year because of his workload. He felt happy to retire. (workload 工作量，好词)Mark 去年因工作量辞职了，退休让他很开心。

◆ 扩展词汇

  • 压力程度递进: under pressure → stressed out → overwhelmed 不堪重负 → burned out 倦怠 → anxious 焦虑 → tense 紧绷
  • 缓解方式: relieve stress · blow off steam 发泄 · take a nap · unwind(→E5) · meditate 冥想(→E2) · me time(→E5)
  • 高分用法: Deadlines really stress me out. / I'm stressed about the exam. / cope with stress(→G5)

◆ 扩展语句

  1. I've been so stressed out lately because of the deadlines.最近因为各种截止日期，我压力大到快崩溃了。
  2. Taking a short nap after lunch helps me stay focused.午饭后小睡一会儿能帮我保持专注。
  3. My parents plan to retire early and travel the world.我父母打算早点退休去环游世界。
  4. Too much stress can cause serious health problems.压力太大会导致严重的健康问题。
  5. I always feel stressed out before an important exam.重要考试前我总是紧张得不行。
  6. Going for a walk is my favorite way to relieve stress.散步是我最喜欢的减压方式。
  7. She was overwhelmed by the heavy workload last month.上个月她被繁重的工作量压得喘不过气。
  8. When I feel tense, I take a few deep breaths to calm down.感到紧绷时，我会深呼吸几次让自己平静下来。
  9. Don't keep your stress to yourself — talk to someone you trust.别把压力憋在心里——和你信任的人聊聊。
  10. He burned out after working on the project for three months straight.连续做了三个月项目后，他累垮了。
  • 💬 What stresses you out the most in your daily life?日常生活中什么最让你感到压力？
  • 💬 Do you think people today are more stressed than before?你认为现在的人比以前压力更大吗？

◆ 口语练习

  • What stresses you out the most?
  • How do you usually deal with stress?

Restaurants and Eating Out 餐厅与外食

Intermediates D3▶

◆ 核心词汇

my treat我请客
ready-to-eat即食的
eat like a horse吃得很多
craving/ˈkreɪvɪŋ/(对某物的)强烈渴望
go DutchAA制、各付各的

◆ 教材关键句

  1. Let's get out for lunch — my treat.我们出去吃午饭吧——我请客。
  2. I love ready-to-eat foods such as deli meats and cheeses. (deli 熟食店)我喜欢熟食肉类和奶酪这类即食食品。
  3. She can eat like a horse and never gain weight. (gain weight = put on weight，见E3)她吃得很多却从不长胖。
  4. She was craving for some chocolate.她当时特别想吃巧克力。
  5. My boyfriend and I always go Dutch.我和男友总是AA制。
⚠️ PDF用法欠妥：crave 是及物动词，应为 She was craving some chocolate 或 She had a craving for some chocolate。

◆ 扩展词汇

  • 买单全家福: my treat / It's on me 我请客 · go Dutch / split the bill AA·分摊 · foot the bill 付账
  • 餐厅流程: book/reserve a table 订位 · order 点餐 · waiter/server 服务员 · tip 小费 · dine in 堂食 · takeaway(英)/takeout(美) 外带
  • 食量表达: eat like a bird 吃得少 · have a sweet tooth 爱吃甜 · grab a bite 随便吃点 · be stuffed 吃饱了 · skip meals 不吃饭

◆ 扩展语句

  1. Let's try that new Italian restaurant tonight — my treat!今晚我们去试试那家新开的意大利餐厅——我请客！
  2. I always keep some ready-to-eat food at home for busy days.我家里总备着一些即食食品，忙的时候吃。
  3. My brother eats like a horse, but he never gains weight.我弟弟吃得很多，但从不长胖。
  4. I have a sudden craving for hotpot tonight.我今晚突然特别想吃火锅。
  5. When we eat out together, we usually go Dutch.我们一起出去吃饭时通常AA制。
  6. Could we split the bill? I didn't bring much cash.我们能分摊账单吗？我没带多少现金。
  7. I booked a table for four at the new restaurant.我在那家新餐厅订了一张四人桌。
  8. Don't order too much — I don't want to waste food.别点太多——我不想浪费食物。
  9. The service was great, so we left a big tip.服务很棒，所以我们给了不少小费。
  10. Let's order takeaway tonight — I'm too tired to cook.今晚点外卖吧——我太累了，不想做饭。
  • 💬 How often do you eat out in a week?你一周出去吃几次饭？
  • 💬 When a friend invites you to dinner, who usually pays?朋友请你吃饭时，通常谁买单？

◆ 口语练习

  • When you eat out with friends, do you go Dutch?
  • Do you have a sweet tooth?

Diets 饮食

Intermediates E3▶

◆ 核心词汇

(dietary) regimen/ˈredʒɪmən/(饮食/锻炼)养生计划
nutrition/nuˈtrɪʃn/营养
nourishment/ˈnɜːrɪʃmənt/滋养、养料
put on weight增重、长胖
lost a lot of weight瘦了很多(反义组)

◆ 教材关键句

  1. Patients maintain a strict dietary regimen. (maintain 坚持)患者坚持严格的饮食计划。
  2. Nutrition and exercise are essential to fitness and health. (be essential to 对…至关重要)营养和运动对健身与健康至关重要。
  3. She collapsed due to lack of proper nourishment. (collapse 晕倒; due to 由于)她因缺乏适当营养而晕倒。
  4. Dolly has put on weight after giving birth.Dolly 生完孩子后长胖了。
  5. I saw Peter yesterday, he's lost a lot of weight.我昨天见到 Peter，他瘦了很多。

◆ 扩展词汇

  • 饮食行为: go on a diet 节食 · balanced diet 均衡饮食 · cut down on 减少摄入 · give up 戒掉 · craving 渴望 · overeat 吃太多 · appetite 食欲
  • 食物类: junk food 垃圾食品 · processed food 加工食品 · organic food 有机食品 · comfort food 治愈系食物 · supplements 保健品
  • 进阶词: metabolism 新陈代谢 · nutrients 营养素 · vegetarian 素食者 · vegan 纯素食者

◆ 扩展语句

  1. I'm trying to cut down on sugar in my diet.我正在努力减少饮食中的糖分。
  2. He put on weight during the holidays because he ate too much.假期里他吃得太多，长胖了。
  3. She has lost weight since she started jogging every day.自从每天开始慢跑，她已经瘦下来了。
  4. Good nutrition is essential for children's growth.良好的营养对孩子的成长至关重要。
  5. I try to eat a balanced diet with plenty of vegetables and fruit.我尽量均衡饮食，多吃蔬菜和水果。
  6. Skipping breakfast is bad for your health.不吃早餐对健康有害。
  7. I've been craving junk food lately, but I know it's not healthy.我最近特别想吃垃圾食品，但我知道那不健康。
  8. Drinking enough water is part of a healthy diet.喝足够的水也是健康饮食的一部分。
  9. My doctor advised me to follow a strict dietary regimen.医生建议我遵循严格的饮食计划。
  10. Processed food usually contains too much salt and fat.加工食品通常含有过多的盐和脂肪。
  • 💬 Have you ever been on a diet? Did it work?你减过肥吗？有效果吗？
  • 💬 I believe a healthy lifestyle matters more than dieting alone.我认为健康的生活方式比单纯节食更重要。

◆ 口语练习

  • Have you ever been on a diet? Did it work?
  • What's your biggest food craving?

Alternative Medicine 另类疗法

Intermediates E2▶

◆ 核心词汇

patient/ˈpeɪʃnt/病人、患者
aromatherapy/əˌroʊməˈθerəpi/芳香疗法
herbal/ˈɜːrbl/草本的、草药的
yoga/ˈjoʊɡə/瑜伽
equilibrium/ˌiːkwɪˈlɪbriəm/平衡(例句好词)

◆ 教材关键句

  1. Most patients nowadays prefer alternative medicine to modern drugs. (prefer A to B 结构!)如今大多数患者比起现代药物更喜欢替代疗法。
  2. Aromatherapy allows your body and mind to achieve equilibrium. (allow sb to do)芳香疗法可以让身体和心灵达到平衡。
  3. Herbal remedies are very effective. (remedy 疗法、药物)草药疗法非常有效。
  4. He has been teaching yoga full time for six years. (现在完成进行时)他已经全职教瑜伽六年了。

◆ 课堂补充

  • herbal = made of herbs；herbs 指有软茎、用于烹饪和医药的植物
  • aromatherapy = 用有好闻气味的天然精油让人感觉更好的疗法

◆ 扩展词汇

  • 疗法类: acupuncture 针灸 · massage 按摩 · meditation 冥想 · TCM 中医 · chiropractic 脊柱按摩疗法
  • 医疗类: treatment 治疗 · therapy 疗法 · side effects 副作用 · chronic 慢性的 · heal 治愈 · alleviate 缓解 · holistic 整体的 · placebo effect 安慰剂效应
  • 句型: I'm skeptical of… 我对…持怀疑态度 · It works for some people, but…

◆ 扩展语句

  1. More and more people are trying alternative medicine these days.现在越来越多的人在尝试替代医学。
  2. Aromatherapy helps me relax after a long day at work.忙碌了一天之后，芳香疗法能帮我放松。
  3. My grandmother swears by herbal remedies for colds.我祖母坚信草药疗法能治感冒。(swear by 深信)
  4. I practice yoga every morning to keep my body and mind in equilibrium.我每天早上练瑜伽，让身心保持平衡。
  5. Some herbal remedies may interact with modern medicines.有些草药疗法可能与现代药物产生相互作用。
  6. Getting enough sleep is the best medicine.睡眠充足是最好的良药。
  7. Acupuncture is another popular form of alternative medicine.针灸是另一种流行的替代疗法。
  8. Regular exercise and a balanced diet can prevent many illnesses.规律运动和均衡饮食能预防很多疾病。
  9. I go for a massage once a month to relieve stress.我每个月去做一次按摩来缓解压力。
  10. You should consult a doctor before trying any alternative treatment.在尝试任何替代疗法之前，你应该先咨询医生。
  • 💬 Do you think alternative medicine actually works, or is it just a placebo?你认为替代医学真的有效，还是只是安慰剂效应？
  • 💬 Prevention is better than cure.预防胜于治疗。

◆ 口语练习

  • Have you ever tried any alternative medicine? Does it work?

Computers and Internet 电脑与网络

Intermediates D4Beginners D2▶

◆ 核心词汇

hard copy纸质打印版
cursor/ˈkɜːrsər/光标

◆ 教材关键句

  1. I usually spend most of my time browsing the internet.我通常大部分时间都在上网浏览。
  2. A laptop would be really useful when I'm working on the train. (would be 含蓄的虚拟假设)在火车上办公时，笔记本电脑会很有用。
  3. We need to pass the hard copies of our reports to our teacher as soon as possible. (as soon as possible 尽快)我们需要尽快把报告的纸质版交给老师。
  4. You can move the cursor either by using the mouse or by using the arrow keys on the keyboard. (either…or…)你可以用鼠标或键盘上的方向键移动光标。
  5. You can request a printout of your phone bills.你可以申请打印一份电话账单。
⚠️ 原PDF "request for a printout" 语法错误：request 及物，不加 for。

◆ 课堂补充

  • soft copy 电子版(课堂补充，与 hard copy 相对)：a digital version of a document viewed on a device
  • I search for some information, watch some videos, and do some coding.我查资料、看视频、写代码。(描述上网活动)
  • I use a desktop monitor in my office for work, and I use a laptop at home.我办公用台式显示器，在家用笔记本。

◆ Beginners 基础补充 (D2)

  • mouse 鼠标 · monitor 显示器 · keyboard 键盘 · headset 耳机
  • There's something wrong with the mouse. (某物出毛病)
  • Have your headset fixed at the computer shop. (have sth fixed 请人修理，使役结构!)

◆ 扩展词汇

  • 硬件: desktop 台式机 · tablet 平板 · charger 充电器 · hard drive 硬盘 · USB drive U盘
  • 上网行为: surf the internet · scroll 刷屏幕 · stream 流媒体 · download/upload · log in/out · bookmark 收藏
  • 高频词: password 密码 · software/hardware · glitch 小故障 · crash 死机 · frozen 卡死 · update 更新 · back up 备份
  • 延伸话题: screen time 屏幕时间 · digital detox 数字排毒 · addicted to phones 手机上瘾 · online shopping · remote work 远程办公

◆ 扩展语句

  1. I spend too much time browsing the internet before bed.我睡前花太多时间刷网页。
  2. Could you send me a soft copy of the report? I don't need a hard copy.你能把报告的电子版发给我吗？我不需要纸质版。
  3. Please print a hard copy of the contract for the meeting.请为会议打印一份纸质合同。
  4. The cursor disappeared when the screen froze.屏幕卡住时，光标就消失了。
  5. My laptop is running slow. I think I need to restart it.我的笔记本电脑运行很慢，我想我需要重启一下。
  6. I always back up my files to the cloud in case my computer crashes.我总是把文件备份到云端，以防电脑死机。
  7. Do you prefer working on a desktop or a laptop?你更喜欢用台式电脑还是笔记本电脑工作？
  8. You should update your software regularly to keep your computer secure.你应该定期更新软件以保证电脑安全。
  9. I got a pop-up ad that I couldn't close.我弹出了一个广告，怎么都关不掉。
  10. The Wi-Fi connection is unstable in my bedroom.我卧室里的 Wi-Fi 连接不稳定。
  • 💬 The internet has changed the way we work, study, and communicate.互联网改变了我们工作、学习和交流的方式。
  • 💬 I think people rely too much on their smartphones these days.我觉得现在人们对智能手机的依赖太严重了。

◆ 口语练习

  • How much time do you spend browsing the internet daily?
  • Could you live without your laptop for a week?

Innovation 创新

Intermediates H3▶

◆ 核心词汇

invention/ɪnˈvenʃn/发明
feature/ˈfiːtʃər/功能、特点
break new ground开创新局面

autonomous/ɔːˈtɑːnəməs/自动的、自主的

◆ 教材关键句

  1. The computer and the Internet are two of the greatest inventions of the modern world.计算机和互联网是现代世界最伟大的两项发明。
  2. The company's most recent phone model has the best features so far.该公司最新的手机型号拥有迄今最好的功能。
  3. Advanced technology allowed them to break new ground in the film industry.先进技术让他们在电影业开了新局。
  4. Studies reveal that people spend around 2 hours on social media daily. (reveal 揭示；串D4屏幕时间)研究显示人们每天在社交媒体上花费约两小时。
