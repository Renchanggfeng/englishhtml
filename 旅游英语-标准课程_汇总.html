高频万能句型总结

把 100 个单元的核心句拆开看，其实只反复使用下面 11 个句型骨架——先掌握骨架，再往里填单词即可举一反三。

I'd like (to) …　我想要……全课程最高频：订票 / 选座 / 订房 / 租车 / 看病全靠它
  • I'd like to book a flight to Rome. / I'd like a window seat, please.　订去罗马的机票 / 要靠窗座位
  • I'd like a room for two nights. / I'd like a wake-up call, please.　住两晚的房间 / 要叫早服务
  • I'd like to rent a car. / I'd like to see a doctor.　租车 / 看医生
Could / Can I have …?　我能要……吗向对方索要东西或服务，点单万能句
  • Could I have a large pizza? / Could I have some more water?　要大号披萨 / 再加点水
  • Can I have a city map? / Can I have a tour brochure, please?　要城市地图 / 旅游手册
  • Could I have a round-trip ticket to Central Park, please?　一张中央公园往返票
Could you …? / Would you …?　您能……吗客气地请对方做事
  • Could you recommend a local dish? / Could you suggest a cheaper hotel?　推荐本地菜 / 便宜点的旅馆
  • Could you hurry to the railroad station? / Would you take a picture of me?　开快点去火车站 / 帮拍张照
  • Could you wrap this as a gift? / Could you write it down in English?　按礼物包装 / 用英语写下来
Do you have …? / Do you carry …?　你们有……吗
  • Do you have a package tour? / Do you have anything on sale?　有跟团游吗 / 有打折商品吗
  • Do you have a twin room? / Do you have this suit in size 7?　有双人房吗 / 这套有7号吗
  • Do you accept credit cards? / Do you carry Italian brand items?　收信用卡吗 / 有意大利品牌吗
Where can I find / get …?　哪里能找到……问地点、要资料
  • Where can I find the check-in counter for CAL? / Where can I find a money changer?　值机柜台 / 兑换处
  • Where can I get a transportation map? / Where can I buy a transfer ticket?　交通图 / 换乘票
How can I get to …? / How do I …?　怎么去 / 怎么做问路 + 问用法
  • How can I get to the boarding gate? / How can I get to the musical theater from here?　去登机口 / 从这里去剧院
  • How do I use the safe? / How do I make an international call?　用保险箱 / 打国际电话
  • How do I take this medicine?　这药怎么服用
How much is / are …?　……多少钱is 接单数、are 接复数
  • How much is the fare to the railroad station? / How much is this?　到火车站车费 / 这件多少钱
  • How much are the tickets for two adults? / How much are those?　两张成人票 / 那些多少钱
  • How much is the mileage charge?　里程费多少
Is there …? / Are there any …?　有……吗问某处是否存在某物
  • Is there a shuttle bus that goes to terminal 1? / Is there a non-smoking section?　有去1号航站楼的接驳车吗 / 有禁烟区吗
  • Are there any available tickets? / Are there any discount tickets?　还有票吗 / 有打折票吗
  • Is there an emergency treatment hospital near here?　附近有急诊医院吗
What time does …? / Until when …?　问时间
  • What time does the tour start? / What time does the musical begin?　观光几点开始 / 音乐剧几点开演
  • Until when is breakfast served? / Until when is check-out?　早餐到几点 / 几点退房
I have a …　我患……/ 我有……描述症状与随身物品
  • I have a fever. / I have a bad headache. / I have a stomachache.　发烧 / 头痛得厉害 / 胃痛
  • I have a piece of luggage to check in. / I have nothing to declare.　一件托运行李 / 无申报物品
I feel … / It hurts …　感觉与疼痛就医描述身体状况
  • I feel nauseous. / I feel like fainting. / I feel cold.　恶心 / 要晕倒 / 发冷
  • It hurts here. / It hurts when I touch it. / It hurts every five minutes.　这里疼 / 一碰就疼 / 每5分钟疼一次
